Deleting your DhanMoney friends mailbox account

DhanMoney keeps your transactions on your phone. The only account it creates is for the friends mailbox, and only if you turn the mailbox on.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Settings → Friends mailbox.
  2. Tap Delete mailbox account, and sign in with Google once more when asked.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account: the email address and user ID stored when you signed in.
  • Your published public keys.
  • Anything waiting in your mailbox, and messages you sent that nobody has collected yet.
  • The list of friends allowed to send to you, and any link invites you left open.
  • The copy of your mailbox key in your Google Drive's private app folder.
  • On your phone: your links to friends and the messages the app collected.

Your transactions, including anything you already saved from a friend, are not touched.

If you no longer have the app

Email <CONTACT ADDRESS> from the Google account you signed in with, and ask for your friends mailbox account to be deleted. Everything listed above that is stored on the server is deleted within 30 days.

The copy of your key in your own Google Drive is yours to remove at any time: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps → DhanMoney → Options → Delete hidden app data.
