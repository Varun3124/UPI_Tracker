DhanMoney privacy policy

Last updated 29 September 2026.

DhanMoney keeps your spending, and what you and your friends owe each other, on your own phone. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

What stays on your phone

Your transactions, accounts, categories, friends, chapters and balances are stored only on your phone. To record payments for you, the app reads bank SMS messages and bank app notifications on the phone, and statements you choose to import. It reads them on the phone and never sends them anywhere.

Google Sign-In

Two optional features ask you to sign in with Google. From your Google account DhanMoney uses only:

  • Your name and email address, for the friends mailbox account described below.
  • Google Drive app data (drive.appdata): a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only DhanMoney can see. It cannot see any of your other files.

DhanMoney's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Backup to Google Drive (optional)

If you turn it on, a copy of the app's data is saved to the hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so you can restore it on a new phone. It goes from your phone straight to your Drive; the developer never receives it. You can remove it at any time in Google Drive on the web: Settings → Manage apps → DhanMoney → Options → Delete hidden app data.

Friends mailbox (optional)

The mailbox lets you send transactions, agreed balances and shared chapters to friends you have linked with. If you turn it on:

  • An account is created with Firebase Authentication (Google), holding your email address and a user ID.
  • Your public keys, and the list of friends allowed to send to you, are stored in Cloud Firestore (Google).
  • Everything you send is encrypted on your phone for the one friend it is for, and signed. The server stores it only until your friend's phone collects it. Neither the developer nor Google can read it.
  • When you invite a friend, the invite carries the name on your Google account, so they can see who it is from.
  • A copy of your mailbox key is kept in your own Google Drive's hidden app folder, so a new phone can read what was sent to you.

What you send is, by design, seen by the friend you send it to: the amounts, the dates, who was involved and your notes on those transactions.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything on your phone. To delete your mailbox account and everything stored for it on the server, see Deleting your friends mailbox account.

Children

DhanMoney is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
